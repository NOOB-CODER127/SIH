IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-086
State Prosecution VERSUS Ajay Patil alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0086 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Ajay Patil, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Vikram Nair to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and Gomti Nagar.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle HR09JK9914 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9899170406.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,690,000 transferred from account ACC2402 belonging to victim Manoj Deshmukh into illicit account ACC9576.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ajay Patil using mobile 9899170406 and co-accused Vikram Nair at number 9999686286.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9316. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
