IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-045
State Prosecution VERSUS Dinesh Deshmukh alias Bunty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0045 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Dinesh Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Bunty, co-operated with accomplice Rohan Deshmukh to execute unauthorized transactions in Vibhuti Khand and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle TS63AB8700 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9869355025.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,880,000 transferred from account ACC3973 belonging to victim Prakash Khan into illicit account ACC8141.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Dinesh Deshmukh using mobile 9869355025 and co-accused Rohan Deshmukh at number 9947333543.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7690. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
