IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-062
State Prosecution VERSUS Salim Sheikh alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0062 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Salim Sheikh, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Arun Shukla to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle MH28CD6383 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9885776373.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,060,000 transferred from account ACC2124 belonging to victim Amit Gupta into illicit account ACC4394.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Salim Sheikh using mobile 9885776373 and co-accused Arun Shukla at number 9937542226.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3417. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
