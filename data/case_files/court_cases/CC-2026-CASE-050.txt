IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-050
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Tiwari alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0050 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Anil Tiwari, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Karan Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Vibhuti Khand.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle TS31JK7274 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9860628276.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,290,000 transferred from account ACC6272 belonging to victim Amit Mishra into illicit account ACC4090.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Tiwari using mobile 9860628276 and co-accused Karan Khan at number 9971115319.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7730. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
