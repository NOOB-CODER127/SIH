IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-052
State Prosecution VERSUS Rohan Sharma alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0052 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Rohan Sharma, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Vijay Ahmed to execute unauthorized transactions in Hyderabad and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle DL81MJ5708 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9875491202.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,260,000 transferred from account ACC1588 belonging to victim Karan Joshi into illicit account ACC2121.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rohan Sharma using mobile 9875491202 and co-accused Vijay Ahmed at number 9982613940.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2480. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
