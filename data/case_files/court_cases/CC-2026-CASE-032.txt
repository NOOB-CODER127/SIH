IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-032
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Mehta alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0032 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Mehta, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Anil Shukla to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mishra seized vehicle DL26AB3496 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9819209801.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,320,000 transferred from account ACC1659 belonging to victim Ramesh Singh into illicit account ACC1508.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Mehta using mobile 9819209801 and co-accused Anil Shukla at number 9997477029.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3068. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
