JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Imran Sheikh, alias 'Raja', who functioned as Phishing Operator.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-5967 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 3,840,000 were illegally transferred from victim Rajesh Malhotra (Account ACC9005) into illicit mule account ACC6245 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Bandra Kurla Complex and Hitec City Phase 2.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9847286793 held by Imran Sheikh (alias Raja) traversing cell towers in Bandra Kurla Complex and Hitec City Phase 2.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Imran Sheikh (alias Raja) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC6245 is confirmed.
