IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-022
State Prosecution VERSUS Sunil Joshi alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0022 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Sunil Joshi, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Karan Sharma to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Shukla seized vehicle HR94EF8149 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9845630292.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,200,000 transferred from account ACC2776 belonging to victim Deepak Mehta into illicit account ACC8119.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sunil Joshi using mobile 9845630292 and co-accused Karan Sharma at number 9915134794.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9379. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
