IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-060
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Yadav alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0060 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Yadav, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Anil Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Banjara Hills.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle MH96MJ3531 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9870887034.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,630,000 transferred from account ACC9850 belonging to victim Manoj Mishra into illicit account ACC7866.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Yadav using mobile 9870887034 and co-accused Anil Verma at number 9959796322.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7797. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
