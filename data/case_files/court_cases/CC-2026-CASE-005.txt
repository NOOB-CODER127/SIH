IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-005
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Sharma alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0005 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Sharma, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Rohan Ahmed to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sheikh seized vehicle HR18CD9830 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9877005685.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,440,000 transferred from account ACC8517 belonging to victim Dinesh Sharma into illicit account ACC3340.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Sharma using mobile 9877005685 and co-accused Rohan Ahmed at number 9963100814.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8019. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
