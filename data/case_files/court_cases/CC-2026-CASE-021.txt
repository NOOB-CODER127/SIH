IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-021
State Prosecution VERSUS Prakash Verma alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0021 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Prakash Verma, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Hyderabad and Aminabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Gupta seized vehicle KA43EF2137 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9840547349.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,050,000 transferred from account ACC1387 belonging to victim Imran Pandey into illicit account ACC4164.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Prakash Verma using mobile 9840547349 and co-accused Ramesh Khan at number 9939920292.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5573. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
