IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-041
State Prosecution VERSUS Salim Deshmukh alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0041 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Salim Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Tiwari to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Tiwari seized vehicle MH49GH8253 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9825898299.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,620,000 transferred from account ACC7818 belonging to victim Dinesh Patil into illicit account ACC4697.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Salim Deshmukh using mobile 9825898299 and co-accused Ramesh Tiwari at number 9984903294.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8025. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
