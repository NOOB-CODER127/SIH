IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-046
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Rao alias Bhaiya & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0046 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Rao, known in criminal records as Bhaiya, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle UP99GH9431 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9821819185.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,320,000 transferred from account ACC5135 belonging to victim Vijay Mishra into illicit account ACC6296.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Rao using mobile 9821819185 and co-accused Rajesh Khan at number 9952213778.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1018. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
