IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-076
State Prosecution VERSUS Suresh Mehta alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0076 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Suresh Mehta, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Hitec City and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Chauhan seized vehicle UP83AB9307 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9815047559.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,060,000 transferred from account ACC7041 belonging to victim Salim Patil into illicit account ACC5703.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Suresh Mehta using mobile 9815047559 and co-accused Ramesh Singh at number 9917635653.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8581. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
