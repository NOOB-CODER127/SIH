IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-031
State Prosecution VERSUS Karan Shukla alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0031 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Karan Shukla, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Dinesh Deshmukh to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Shukla seized vehicle KA42CD8449 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9871968116.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,770,000 transferred from account ACC1823 belonging to victim Salim Kumar into illicit account ACC5262.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Karan Shukla using mobile 9871968116 and co-accused Dinesh Deshmukh at number 9934391257.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6529. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
