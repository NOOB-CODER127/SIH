IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-083
State Prosecution VERSUS Suresh Ahmed alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0083 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Suresh Ahmed, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Rohan Pandey to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Chauhan seized vehicle KA07GH9224 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9839801436.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,010,000 transferred from account ACC6727 belonging to victim Ajay Kumar into illicit account ACC1436.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Suresh Ahmed using mobile 9839801436 and co-accused Rohan Pandey at number 9970549373.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4862. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
