IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-061
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Kumar alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0061 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Arun Kumar, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sheikh seized vehicle TS46AB7523 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9858678896.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,510,000 transferred from account ACC7018 belonging to victim Sanjay Pandey into illicit account ACC9920.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Kumar using mobile 9858678896 and co-accused Ramesh Singh at number 9923335151.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4109. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
