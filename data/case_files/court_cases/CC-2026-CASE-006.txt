IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-006
State Prosecution VERSUS Rahul Gupta alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0006 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Rahul Gupta, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Sunil Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Ahmed seized vehicle KA77GH9669 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9831472420.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,380,000 transferred from account ACC2040 belonging to victim Ramesh Verma into illicit account ACC7304.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rahul Gupta using mobile 9831472420 and co-accused Sunil Khan at number 9966661351.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1188. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
