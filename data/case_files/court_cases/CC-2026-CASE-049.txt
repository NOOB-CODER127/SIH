IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-049
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Gupta alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0049 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Anil Gupta, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Karan Tiwari to execute unauthorized transactions in Vibhuti Khand and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Joshi seized vehicle HR65EF1042 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9828321839.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,540,000 transferred from account ACC5526 belonging to victim Imran Nair into illicit account ACC7798.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Gupta using mobile 9828321839 and co-accused Karan Tiwari at number 9919369750.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5891. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
