IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-059
State Prosecution VERSUS Rohan Deshmukh alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0059 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Rohan Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Rahul Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Gupta seized vehicle TS60MJ5951 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9885350823.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,420,000 transferred from account ACC4626 belonging to victim Anil Shukla into illicit account ACC2035.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rohan Deshmukh using mobile 9885350823 and co-accused Rahul Verma at number 9954024257.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7689. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
