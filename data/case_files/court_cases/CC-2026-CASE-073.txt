IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-073
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Ahmed alias Bhaiya & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0073 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Ahmed, known in criminal records as Bhaiya, co-operated with accomplice Dinesh Kumar to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle MH59MJ6928 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9864362516.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 550,000 transferred from account ACC6321 belonging to victim Vijay Kumar into illicit account ACC3815.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Ahmed using mobile 9864362516 and co-accused Dinesh Kumar at number 9912799319.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8155. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
