IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-025
State Prosecution VERSUS Amit Pandey alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0025 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Amit Pandey, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Sheikh to execute unauthorized transactions in New Delhi and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Chauhan seized vehicle DL87EF4680 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9848089166.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,220,000 transferred from account ACC6491 belonging to victim Anil Rao into illicit account ACC2530.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Amit Pandey using mobile 9848089166 and co-accused Sanjay Sheikh at number 9979182797.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4262. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
