IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-010
State Prosecution VERSUS Manoj Gupta alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0010 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Manoj Gupta, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Amit Shukla to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle UP05EF2160 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9810965138.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,730,000 transferred from account ACC1964 belonging to victim Karan Gupta into illicit account ACC4750.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Manoj Gupta using mobile 9810965138 and co-accused Amit Shukla at number 9919528530.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4899. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
