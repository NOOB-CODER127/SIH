IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-081
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Joshi alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0081 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Arun Joshi, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Prakash Rao to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Banjara Hills.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle TS58CD9749 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9858895439.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,320,000 transferred from account ACC8277 belonging to victim Prakash Pandey into illicit account ACC5668.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Joshi using mobile 9858895439 and co-accused Prakash Rao at number 9941880763.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6070. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
