IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-043
State Prosecution VERSUS Suresh Pandey alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0043 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Suresh Pandey, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Vibhuti Khand.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle DL10AB3718 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9840173413.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,150,000 transferred from account ACC3441 belonging to victim Imran Kumar into illicit account ACC5352.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Suresh Pandey using mobile 9840173413 and co-accused Manoj Mehta at number 9996924061.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6039. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
