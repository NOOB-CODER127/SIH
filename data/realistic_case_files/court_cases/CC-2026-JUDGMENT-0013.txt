JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by ACP Crime Branch A.K. Varma centers on accused Imran Sheikh, alias 'Raja', who functioned as Phishing Operator.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-8540 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 6,460,000 were illegally transferred from victim Kunal Joshi (Account ACC9003) into illicit mule account ACC6140 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Aminabad Bazaar and Charbagh Railway Station.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9827549517 held by Imran Sheikh (alias Raja) traversing cell towers in Aminabad Bazaar and Charbagh Railway Station.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Imran Sheikh (alias Raja) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC6140 is confirmed.
