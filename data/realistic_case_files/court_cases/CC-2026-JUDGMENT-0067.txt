JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector S.K. Pandey centers on accused Deepak Verma, alias 'Doctor', who functioned as Logistics Driver.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-5141 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 4,440,000 were illegally transferred from victim Rajesh Malhotra (Account ACC9005) into illicit mule account ACC7229 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Hazratganj Market and Charbagh Railway Station.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9854802773 held by Deepak Verma (alias Doctor) traversing cell towers in Hazratganj Market and Charbagh Railway Station.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Deepak Verma (alias Doctor) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC7229 is confirmed.
