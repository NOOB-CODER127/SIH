IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-088
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Rao alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0088 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Anil Rao, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Salim Pandey to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle MH51JK8756 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9836772387.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,390,000 transferred from account ACC6170 belonging to victim Suresh Sheikh into illicit account ACC6080.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Rao using mobile 9836772387 and co-accused Salim Pandey at number 9915624520.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1599. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
