IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-037
State Prosecution VERSUS Sunil Sheikh alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0037 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Sunil Sheikh, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Kumar to execute unauthorized transactions in Charbagh Railway Station and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Khan seized vehicle UP38CD7625 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9896691619.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,640,000 transferred from account ACC8342 belonging to victim Karan Gupta into illicit account ACC1282.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sunil Sheikh using mobile 9896691619 and co-accused Manoj Kumar at number 9975876924.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4986. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
