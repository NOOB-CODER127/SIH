IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-028
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Pandey alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0028 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Anil Pandey, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Chauhan seized vehicle HR41JK2320 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9820399639.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 800,000 transferred from account ACC4841 belonging to victim Anil Gupta into illicit account ACC5451.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Pandey using mobile 9820399639 and co-accused Manoj Mehta at number 9948349783.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3471. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
