IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-105
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Kumar alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0105 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Kumar, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Suresh Shukla to execute unauthorized transactions in New Delhi and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle MH62CD9589 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9887641510.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,320,000 transferred from account ACC4728 belonging to victim Anil Mehta into illicit account ACC1841.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Kumar using mobile 9887641510 and co-accused Suresh Shukla at number 9987528776.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7229. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
