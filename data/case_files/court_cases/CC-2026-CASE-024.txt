IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-024
State Prosecution VERSUS Suresh Kumar alias Bhaiya & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0024 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Suresh Kumar, known in criminal records as Bhaiya, co-operated with accomplice Vijay Patil to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mishra seized vehicle MH01EF5700 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9860888017.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,170,000 transferred from account ACC5930 belonging to victim Ajay Sheikh into illicit account ACC7653.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Suresh Kumar using mobile 9860888017 and co-accused Vijay Patil at number 9933357554.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8043. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
