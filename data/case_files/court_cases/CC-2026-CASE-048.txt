IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-048
State Prosecution VERSUS Prakash Patil alias Pandit & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0048 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Prakash Patil, known in criminal records as Pandit, co-operated with accomplice Vikram Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Pune and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Kumar seized vehicle KA92EF9337 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9825228493.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,710,000 transferred from account ACC2924 belonging to victim Salim Gupta into illicit account ACC3522.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Prakash Patil using mobile 9825228493 and co-accused Vikram Mishra at number 9971934487.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5479. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
