JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Manoj Gupta, alias 'Munna', who functioned as Financial Launderer.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-3342 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 3,310,000 were illegally transferred from victim Kunal Joshi (Account ACC9003) into illicit mule account ACC8733 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Banjara Hills Road No 12 and Banjara Hills Road No 12.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9898630760 held by Manoj Gupta (alias Munna) traversing cell towers in Banjara Hills Road No 12 and Banjara Hills Road No 12.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Manoj Gupta (alias Munna) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC8733 is confirmed.
