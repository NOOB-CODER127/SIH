IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-002
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Deshmukh alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0002 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Deshmukh, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Vikram Rao to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Kumar seized vehicle UP46EF5333 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9855176955.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,230,000 transferred from account ACC2519 belonging to victim Amit Mehta into illicit account ACC7224.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Deshmukh using mobile 9855176955 and co-accused Vikram Rao at number 9923718431.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1711. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
