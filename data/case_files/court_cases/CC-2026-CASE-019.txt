IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-019
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Mishra alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0019 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Mishra, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Bangalore and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Shukla seized vehicle MH71CD8041 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9830005727.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 750,000 transferred from account ACC1590 belonging to victim Deepak Ahmed into illicit account ACC7049.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Mishra using mobile 9830005727 and co-accused Rajesh Mishra at number 9983227889.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1685. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
