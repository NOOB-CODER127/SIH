IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-058
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Patil alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0058 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Anil Patil, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Amit Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Pandey seized vehicle KA02EF9791 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9863339667.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 730,000 transferred from account ACC6992 belonging to victim Salim Tiwari into illicit account ACC2479.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Patil using mobile 9863339667 and co-accused Amit Verma at number 9984801233.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8451. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
