JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector Cyber Cell Priya Rao centers on accused Salim Ahmed, alias 'Salim Bhai', who functioned as Stolen Goods Fence.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-9593 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 8,000,000 were illegally transferred from victim Rohan Mehta (Account ACC9001) into illicit mule account ACC7677 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Hitec City Phase 2 and Aliganj Sector C.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9863122273 held by Salim Ahmed (alias Salim Bhai) traversing cell towers in Hitec City Phase 2 and Aliganj Sector C.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Salim Ahmed (alias Salim Bhai) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC7677 is confirmed.
