IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-098
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Deshmukh alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0098 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Deshmukh, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Imran Deshmukh to execute unauthorized transactions in Hitec City and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Kumar seized vehicle KA32MJ7653 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9894176528.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,620,000 transferred from account ACC5745 belonging to victim Arun Shukla into illicit account ACC5949.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Deshmukh using mobile 9894176528 and co-accused Imran Deshmukh at number 9983226170.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8430. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
