JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Imran Sheikh, alias 'Raja', who functioned as Phishing Operator.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-9167 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 3,310,000 were illegally transferred from victim Kunal Joshi (Account ACC9003) into illicit mule account ACC2283 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Bandra Kurla Complex and Aminabad Bazaar.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9843376198 held by Imran Sheikh (alias Raja) traversing cell towers in Bandra Kurla Complex and Aminabad Bazaar.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Imran Sheikh (alias Raja) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC2283 is confirmed.
