IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-013
State Prosecution VERSUS Karan Shukla alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0013 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Karan Shukla, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Rao to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Kumar seized vehicle UP50EF8454 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9863826716.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,560,000 transferred from account ACC3697 belonging to victim Anil Khan into illicit account ACC7209.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Karan Shukla using mobile 9863826716 and co-accused Manoj Rao at number 9917869910.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7930. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
