IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-017
State Prosecution VERSUS Manoj Singh alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0017 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Manoj Singh, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Prakash Pandey to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mishra seized vehicle DL34AB2753 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9880993218.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,900,000 transferred from account ACC5905 belonging to victim Suresh Nair into illicit account ACC2697.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Manoj Singh using mobile 9880993218 and co-accused Prakash Pandey at number 9911049999.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3546. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
