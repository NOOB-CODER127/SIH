IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-091
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Singh alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0091 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Singh, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Hyderabad and Vibhuti Khand.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle UP17AB4866 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9831473054.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,080,000 transferred from account ACC3339 belonging to victim Salim Joshi into illicit account ACC8179.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Singh using mobile 9831473054 and co-accused Rajesh Mehta at number 9914179623.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7001. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
