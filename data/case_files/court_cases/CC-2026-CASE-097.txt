IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-097
State Prosecution VERSUS Suresh Ahmed alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0097 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Suresh Ahmed, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Imran Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Vibhuti Khand.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mehta seized vehicle DL85JK6531 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9822539748.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,410,000 transferred from account ACC3762 belonging to victim Ajay Patil into illicit account ACC3526.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Suresh Ahmed using mobile 9822539748 and co-accused Imran Mishra at number 9977454225.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1674. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
