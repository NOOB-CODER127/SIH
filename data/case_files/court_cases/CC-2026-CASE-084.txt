IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-084
State Prosecution VERSUS Rahul Gupta alias Bunty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0084 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Rahul Gupta, known in criminal records as Bunty, co-operated with accomplice Amit Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Aminabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle MH01AB1311 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9873411827.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,410,000 transferred from account ACC8685 belonging to victim Ajay Tiwari into illicit account ACC8349.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rahul Gupta using mobile 9873411827 and co-accused Amit Verma at number 9928628439.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4533. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
