IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-003
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Joshi alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0003 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Joshi, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Karan Patil to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle UP30AB7227 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9819335534.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,520,000 transferred from account ACC4733 belonging to victim Rahul Deshmukh into illicit account ACC5741.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Joshi using mobile 9819335534 and co-accused Karan Patil at number 9916150444.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8428. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
