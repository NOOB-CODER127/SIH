IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-012
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Yadav alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0012 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Yadav, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Amit Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Vibhuti Khand and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Gupta seized vehicle KA32AB8260 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9828814949.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,230,000 transferred from account ACC4006 belonging to victim Imran Sheikh into illicit account ACC5563.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Yadav using mobile 9828814949 and co-accused Amit Verma at number 9966623995.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2604. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
