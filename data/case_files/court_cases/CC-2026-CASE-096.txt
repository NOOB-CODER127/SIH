IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-096
State Prosecution VERSUS Sunil Singh alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0096 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sunil Singh, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Karan Deshmukh to execute unauthorized transactions in Vibhuti Khand and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Rao seized vehicle HR22CD6286 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9817437386.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,880,000 transferred from account ACC9488 belonging to victim Ajay Kumar into illicit account ACC3507.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sunil Singh using mobile 9817437386 and co-accused Karan Deshmukh at number 9926664623.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4694. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
