IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-070
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Pandey alias Bhaiya & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0070 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Pandey, known in criminal records as Bhaiya, co-operated with accomplice Prakash Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle TS11EF8259 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9817061037.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,480,000 transferred from account ACC3579 belonging to victim Amit Deshmukh into illicit account ACC1815.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Pandey using mobile 9817061037 and co-accused Prakash Singh at number 9930881581.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7947. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
