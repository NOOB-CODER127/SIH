IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-100
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Verma alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0100 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Verma, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Gupta to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Banjara Hills.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Tiwari seized vehicle HR74MJ6123 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9892594562.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,070,000 transferred from account ACC5067 belonging to victim Deepak Yadav into illicit account ACC3616.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Verma using mobile 9892594562 and co-accused Sanjay Gupta at number 9966121095.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3610. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
