JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by ACP Crime Branch A.K. Varma centers on accused Ajay Tiwari, alias 'Pandit', who functioned as Syndicate Henchman.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-4458 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 3,000,000 were illegally transferred from victim Priya Singh (Account ACC9002) into illicit mule account ACC2704 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Gomti Nagar Extension and Charbagh Railway Station.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9855350760 held by Ajay Tiwari (alias Pandit) traversing cell towers in Gomti Nagar Extension and Charbagh Railway Station.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Ajay Tiwari (alias Pandit) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC2704 is confirmed.
