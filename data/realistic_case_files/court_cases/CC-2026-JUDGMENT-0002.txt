JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Salim Ahmed, alias 'Salim Bhai', who functioned as Stolen Goods Fence.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-9183 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 1,020,000 were illegally transferred from victim Kunal Joshi (Account ACC9003) into illicit mule account ACC8968 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Hazratganj Market and Charbagh Railway Station.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9883931843 held by Salim Ahmed (alias Salim Bhai) traversing cell towers in Hazratganj Market and Charbagh Railway Station.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Salim Ahmed (alias Salim Bhai) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC8968 is confirmed.
