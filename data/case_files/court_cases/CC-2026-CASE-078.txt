IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-078
State Prosecution VERSUS Sunil Nair alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0078 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sunil Nair, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Rahul Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Hyderabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle HR13JK7333 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9890246882.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,550,000 transferred from account ACC6482 belonging to victim Vijay Kumar into illicit account ACC2079.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sunil Nair using mobile 9890246882 and co-accused Rahul Singh at number 9917028380.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5128. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
