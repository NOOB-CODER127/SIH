IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-018
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Sheikh alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0018 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Arun Sheikh, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Amit Sheikh to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mehta seized vehicle UP01EF3143 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9816818112.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,360,000 transferred from account ACC7939 belonging to victim Sunil Khan into illicit account ACC5533.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Sheikh using mobile 9816818112 and co-accused Amit Sheikh at number 9922388090.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5291. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
