IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-085
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Chauhan alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0085 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Arun Chauhan, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Deepak Patil to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Gupta seized vehicle UP64JK9782 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9866348030.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 180,000 transferred from account ACC8489 belonging to victim Suresh Verma into illicit account ACC2029.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Chauhan using mobile 9866348030 and co-accused Deepak Patil at number 9995758649.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9443. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
