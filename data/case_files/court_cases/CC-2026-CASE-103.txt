IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-103
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Verma alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0103 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Arun Verma, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Deepak Joshi to execute unauthorized transactions in Hyderabad and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle MH06AB1637 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9829363237.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 800,000 transferred from account ACC9341 belonging to victim Amit Mishra into illicit account ACC7576.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Verma using mobile 9829363237 and co-accused Deepak Joshi at number 9936717096.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6456. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
