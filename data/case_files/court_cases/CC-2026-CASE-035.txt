IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-035
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Singh alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0035 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Singh, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Tiwari to execute unauthorized transactions in New Delhi and Aminabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Kumar seized vehicle KA94JK5397 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9892666356.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,750,000 transferred from account ACC8921 belonging to victim Prakash Chauhan into illicit account ACC8616.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Singh using mobile 9892666356 and co-accused Sanjay Tiwari at number 9982232344.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5022. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
