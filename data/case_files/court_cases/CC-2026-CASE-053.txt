IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-053
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Pandey alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0053 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Pandey, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Suresh Shukla to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Hyderabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle MH53JK3950 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9860310319.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 970,000 transferred from account ACC3385 belonging to victim Amit Yadav into illicit account ACC5000.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Pandey using mobile 9860310319 and co-accused Suresh Shukla at number 9967805802.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3868. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
