IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-065
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Khan alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0065 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Khan, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Ahmed to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Ahmed seized vehicle KA77JK5831 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9826937148.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,420,000 transferred from account ACC9984 belonging to victim Amit Nair into illicit account ACC6327.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Khan using mobile 9826937148 and co-accused Sanjay Ahmed at number 9946912022.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9282. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
