JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector R.K. Singh centers on accused Prakash Rao, alias 'PK', who functioned as Cyber Crime Mastermind.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-4501 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 3,160,000 were illegally transferred from victim Priya Singh (Account ACC9002) into illicit mule account ACC2693 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Aminabad Bazaar and Aliganj Sector C.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9878332224 held by Prakash Rao (alias PK) traversing cell towers in Aminabad Bazaar and Aliganj Sector C.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Prakash Rao (alias PK) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC2693 is confirmed.
