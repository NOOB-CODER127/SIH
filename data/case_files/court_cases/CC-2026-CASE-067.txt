IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-067
State Prosecution VERSUS Salim Ahmed alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0067 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Salim Ahmed, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle KA46MJ7929 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9885374852.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,540,000 transferred from account ACC6400 belonging to victim Ajay Mishra into illicit account ACC3002.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Salim Ahmed using mobile 9885374852 and co-accused Rajesh Verma at number 9985387867.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1842. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
