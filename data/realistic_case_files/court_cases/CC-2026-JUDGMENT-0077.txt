JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector R.K. Singh centers on accused Prakash Rao, alias 'PK', who functioned as Cyber Crime Mastermind.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-3933 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 800,000 were illegally transferred from victim Kunal Joshi (Account ACC9003) into illicit mule account ACC5733 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Aliganj Sector C and Aliganj Sector C.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9882338494 held by Prakash Rao (alias PK) traversing cell towers in Aliganj Sector C and Aliganj Sector C.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Prakash Rao (alias PK) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC5733 is confirmed.
