IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-089
State Prosecution VERSUS Rahul Yadav alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0089 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Rahul Yadav, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Amit Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Bangalore and Banjara Hills.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle KA89GH7018 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9869020451.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,830,000 transferred from account ACC6554 belonging to victim Rahul Kumar into illicit account ACC4060.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rahul Yadav using mobile 9869020451 and co-accused Amit Mehta at number 9961894582.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9507. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
