IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-069
State Prosecution VERSUS Rohan Shukla alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0069 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Rohan Shukla, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Rohan Patil to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle DL95MJ1502 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9829226889.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 560,000 transferred from account ACC7566 belonging to victim Rahul Shukla into illicit account ACC2140.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rohan Shukla using mobile 9829226889 and co-accused Rohan Patil at number 9986010330.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9691. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
