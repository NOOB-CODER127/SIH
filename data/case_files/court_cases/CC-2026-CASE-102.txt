IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-102
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Tiwari alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0102 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Tiwari, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Rahul Joshi to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle UP50JK8378 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9885405783.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,010,000 transferred from account ACC6939 belonging to victim Ramesh Joshi into illicit account ACC5728.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Tiwari using mobile 9885405783 and co-accused Rahul Joshi at number 9936126192.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5582. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
