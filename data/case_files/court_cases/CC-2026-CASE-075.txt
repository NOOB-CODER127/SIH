IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-075
State Prosecution VERSUS Ajay Mehta alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0075 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Ajay Mehta, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Prakash Nair to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Tiwari seized vehicle TS41CD8205 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9876319264.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 440,000 transferred from account ACC8354 belonging to victim Salim Rao into illicit account ACC6295.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ajay Mehta using mobile 9876319264 and co-accused Prakash Nair at number 9972270726.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8682. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
