JUDGMENT
SANJIV KHANNA, J.
1. Leave granted. This appeal challenges the order dated 14.02.2024 passed by the High Court of Judicature at Allahabad in Criminal Miscellaneous Bail Application No. 8920 of 2024.
2. The prosecution case is rooted in FIR No. 2026-0041 registered under Sections 379, 411, and 120-B of the Indian Penal Code, 1860, at Police Station Hazratganj, Lucknow. The allegation involves a sophisticated inter-state vehicle theft and extortion enterprise operated by accused Vikram Shukla (P001), alias Bunty, in conspiracy with Manoj Gupta (P020), alias Munna.
3. Investigating Officer Inspector S.K. Pandey conducted raids in Hazratganj and Aminabad, recovering vehicle registration UP32CD7788 and tracking call detail record (CDR) interactions on mobile 9876543210.
4. Financial investigation by the Enforcement Directorate established illicit fund transfers totaling Rs. 14,50,000 from victim Rohan Mehta account ACC9001 into mule account ACC3003. Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance alert STR-2026-0033.
5. In view of the gravity of offences and risk of tampering with evidence, the High Court order granting bail is set aside. Appeal allowed.
