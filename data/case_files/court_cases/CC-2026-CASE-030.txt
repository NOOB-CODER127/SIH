IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-030
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Mishra alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0030 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Mishra, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Karan Nair to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Banjara Hills.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Gupta seized vehicle TS87GH3704 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9875181765.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,400,000 transferred from account ACC7371 belonging to victim Anil Gupta into illicit account ACC6507.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Mishra using mobile 9875181765 and co-accused Karan Nair at number 9913895645.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8657. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
