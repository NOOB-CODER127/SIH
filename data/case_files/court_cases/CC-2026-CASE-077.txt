IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-077
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Pandey alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0077 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Pandey, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Imran Sharma to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Lucknow.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle MH83CD1641 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9870468188.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,360,000 transferred from account ACC6626 belonging to victim Anil Chauhan into illicit account ACC2381.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Pandey using mobile 9870468188 and co-accused Imran Sharma at number 9923891786.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8172. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
