IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-020
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Singh alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0020 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Singh, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle KA04CD6442 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9830743797.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,100,000 transferred from account ACC3662 belonging to victim Karan Kumar into illicit account ACC3900.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Singh using mobile 9830743797 and co-accused Manoj Verma at number 9941774346.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7745. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
