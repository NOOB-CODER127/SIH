JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector Cyber Cell Priya Rao centers on accused Suresh Patil, alias 'Subhedar', who functioned as Hawala Broker.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-8436 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 3,230,000 were illegally transferred from victim Sunil Agrawal (Account ACC9006) into illicit mule account ACC2618 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Hitec City Phase 2 and Gomti Nagar Extension.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9853402284 held by Suresh Patil (alias Subhedar) traversing cell towers in Hitec City Phase 2 and Gomti Nagar Extension.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Suresh Patil (alias Subhedar) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC2618 is confirmed.
