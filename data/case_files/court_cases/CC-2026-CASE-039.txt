IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-039
State Prosecution VERSUS Prakash Sheikh alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0039 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Prakash Sheikh, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Anil Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle DL39AB9751 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9836054020.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 740,000 transferred from account ACC4727 belonging to victim Sunil Nair into illicit account ACC6912.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Prakash Sheikh using mobile 9836054020 and co-accused Anil Mehta at number 9949761527.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5494. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
