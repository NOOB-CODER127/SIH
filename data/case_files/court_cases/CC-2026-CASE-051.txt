IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-051
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Joshi alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0051 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Anil Joshi, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Ajay Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Hyderabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle KA02MJ3361 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9823470708.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,190,000 transferred from account ACC2633 belonging to victim Ajay Chauhan into illicit account ACC9617.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Joshi using mobile 9823470708 and co-accused Ajay Khan at number 9969096899.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3529. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
