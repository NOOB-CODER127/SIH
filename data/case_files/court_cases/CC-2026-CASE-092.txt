IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-092
State Prosecution VERSUS Dinesh Yadav alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0092 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Dinesh Yadav, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Ajay Pandey to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle KA41MJ5566 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9887030477.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,370,000 transferred from account ACC9674 belonging to victim Rahul Pandey into illicit account ACC7012.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Dinesh Yadav using mobile 9887030477 and co-accused Ajay Pandey at number 9928481124.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2858. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
