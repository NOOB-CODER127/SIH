IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-079
State Prosecution VERSUS Amit Ahmed alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0079 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Amit Ahmed, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Ajay Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle DL86MJ9624 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9821370793.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 570,000 transferred from account ACC7171 belonging to victim Vijay Tiwari into illicit account ACC6381.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Amit Ahmed using mobile 9821370793 and co-accused Ajay Singh at number 9951551377.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7937. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
