IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-015
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Ahmed alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0015 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Arun Ahmed, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Joshi to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle TS75JK9565 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9815334035.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,710,000 transferred from account ACC2343 belonging to victim Dinesh Gupta into illicit account ACC7868.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Ahmed using mobile 9815334035 and co-accused Manoj Joshi at number 9993131979.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5272. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
