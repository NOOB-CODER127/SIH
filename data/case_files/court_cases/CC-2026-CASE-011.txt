IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-011
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Sheikh alias Pandit & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0011 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Anil Sheikh, known in criminal records as Pandit, co-operated with accomplice Ajay Deshmukh to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle TS56EF7939 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9864634663.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,200,000 transferred from account ACC2545 belonging to victim Anil Gupta into illicit account ACC2588.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Sheikh using mobile 9864634663 and co-accused Ajay Deshmukh at number 9935556386.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8651. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
