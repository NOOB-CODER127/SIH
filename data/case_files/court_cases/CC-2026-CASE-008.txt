IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-008
State Prosecution VERSUS Prakash Mishra alias Pandit & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0008 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Prakash Mishra, known in criminal records as Pandit, co-operated with accomplice Vikram Nair to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Bangalore.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle DL73AB2403 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9858718453.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,840,000 transferred from account ACC4923 belonging to victim Anil Shukla into illicit account ACC1949.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Prakash Mishra using mobile 9858718453 and co-accused Vikram Nair at number 9951273847.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8962. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
