IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-007
State Prosecution VERSUS Karan Mehta alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0007 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Karan Mehta, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Amit Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Hitec City and Lucknow.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle UP81EF9317 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9845351479.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,210,000 transferred from account ACC3927 belonging to victim Rajesh Rao into illicit account ACC9317.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Karan Mehta using mobile 9845351479 and co-accused Amit Verma at number 9977187530.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4258. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
