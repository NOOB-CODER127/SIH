IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-071
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Kumar alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0071 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Kumar, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Imran Chauhan to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Aminabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle UP29GH1897 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9889609131.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 130,000 transferred from account ACC9633 belonging to victim Rahul Kumar into illicit account ACC6053.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Kumar using mobile 9889609131 and co-accused Imran Chauhan at number 9975314742.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4349. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
