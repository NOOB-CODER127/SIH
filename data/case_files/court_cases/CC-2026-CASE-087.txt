IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-087
State Prosecution VERSUS Vijay Khan alias Bunty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0087 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Vijay Khan, known in criminal records as Bunty, co-operated with accomplice Vijay Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mehta seized vehicle DL06EF9945 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9856278607.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,830,000 transferred from account ACC2698 belonging to victim Vikram Singh into illicit account ACC6447.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Vijay Khan using mobile 9856278607 and co-accused Vijay Singh at number 9942363373.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3868. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
