IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-036
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Gupta alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0036 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Gupta, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Dinesh Nair to execute unauthorized transactions in Hitec City and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Joshi seized vehicle HR34EF5581 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9853622663.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,600,000 transferred from account ACC8988 belonging to victim Amit Shukla into illicit account ACC4475.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Gupta using mobile 9853622663 and co-accused Dinesh Nair at number 9934406132.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5526. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
