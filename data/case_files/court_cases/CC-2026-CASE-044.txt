IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-044
State Prosecution VERSUS Dinesh Patil alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0044 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Dinesh Patil, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Deepak Pandey to execute unauthorized transactions in Vibhuti Khand and Vibhuti Khand.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle KA95EF5102 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9876276072.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 230,000 transferred from account ACC2317 belonging to victim Vijay Mehta into illicit account ACC1653.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Dinesh Patil using mobile 9876276072 and co-accused Deepak Pandey at number 9968755000.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2496. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
