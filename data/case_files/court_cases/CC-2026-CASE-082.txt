IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-082
State Prosecution VERSUS Rahul Deshmukh alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0082 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Rahul Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Patil to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and Gomti Nagar.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Joshi seized vehicle UP83GH7081 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9836802975.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,930,000 transferred from account ACC3267 belonging to victim Sunil Chauhan into illicit account ACC5096.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rahul Deshmukh using mobile 9836802975 and co-accused Sanjay Patil at number 9991156554.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2680. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
