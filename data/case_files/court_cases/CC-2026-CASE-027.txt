IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-027
State Prosecution VERSUS Prakash Chauhan alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0027 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Prakash Chauhan, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Gupta seized vehicle KA79MJ9270 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9872363359.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,280,000 transferred from account ACC9702 belonging to victim Deepak Pandey into illicit account ACC6198.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Prakash Chauhan using mobile 9872363359 and co-accused Ramesh Mishra at number 9999600766.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9976. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
