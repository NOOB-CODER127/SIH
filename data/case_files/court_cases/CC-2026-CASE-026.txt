IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-026
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Gupta alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0026 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Gupta, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Arun Ahmed to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Pandey seized vehicle DL84MJ1090 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9861536717.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,670,000 transferred from account ACC7547 belonging to victim Rajesh Deshmukh into illicit account ACC4997.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Gupta using mobile 9861536717 and co-accused Arun Ahmed at number 9976354180.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2746. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
