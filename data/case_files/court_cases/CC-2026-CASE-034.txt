IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-034
State Prosecution VERSUS Manoj Verma alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0034 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Manoj Verma, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Suresh Nair to execute unauthorized transactions in Hyderabad and Vibhuti Khand.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle MH83EF1207 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9867495923.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,440,000 transferred from account ACC7071 belonging to victim Dinesh Yadav into illicit account ACC2129.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Manoj Verma using mobile 9867495923 and co-accused Suresh Nair at number 9998787891.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7882. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
