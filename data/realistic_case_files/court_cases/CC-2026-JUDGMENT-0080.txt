JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector S.K. Pandey centers on accused Ramesh Yadav, alias 'Chotu', who functioned as Lieutenant / Extortionist.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-7887 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 2,890,000 were illegally transferred from victim Sunil Agrawal (Account ACC9006) into illicit mule account ACC3460 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Gomti Nagar Extension and Connaught Place Inner Circle.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9893782848 held by Ramesh Yadav (alias Chotu) traversing cell towers in Gomti Nagar Extension and Connaught Place Inner Circle.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Ramesh Yadav (alias Chotu) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC3460 is confirmed.
