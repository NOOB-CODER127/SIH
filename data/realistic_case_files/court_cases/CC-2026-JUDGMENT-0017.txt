JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector S.K. Pandey centers on accused Manoj Gupta, alias 'Munna', who functioned as Financial Launderer.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-7630 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 1,380,000 were illegally transferred from victim Rajesh Malhotra (Account ACC9005) into illicit mule account ACC3971 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Hitec City Phase 2 and Bandra Kurla Complex.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9829920241 held by Manoj Gupta (alias Munna) traversing cell towers in Hitec City Phase 2 and Bandra Kurla Complex.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Manoj Gupta (alias Munna) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC3971 is confirmed.
