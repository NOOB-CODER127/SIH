IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-064
State Prosecution VERSUS Salim Khan alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0064 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Salim Khan, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Rahul Chauhan to execute unauthorized transactions in Vibhuti Khand and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle KA83AB1996 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9892558242.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,550,000 transferred from account ACC9543 belonging to victim Deepak Pandey into illicit account ACC5941.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Salim Khan using mobile 9892558242 and co-accused Rahul Chauhan at number 9915820356.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7580. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
