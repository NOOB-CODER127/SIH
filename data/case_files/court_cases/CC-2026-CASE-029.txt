IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-029
State Prosecution VERSUS Ajay Sheikh alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0029 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Ajay Sheikh, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Kumar to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and Lucknow.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle MH49GH1096 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9837760841.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,920,000 transferred from account ACC7381 belonging to victim Karan Pandey into illicit account ACC1320.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ajay Sheikh using mobile 9837760841 and co-accused Rajesh Kumar at number 9966390708.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6763. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
