IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-038
State Prosecution VERSUS Dinesh Singh alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0038 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Dinesh Singh, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Karan Chauhan to execute unauthorized transactions in Hyderabad and Hyderabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle DL16MJ4155 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9870900658.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,710,000 transferred from account ACC6023 belonging to victim Rajesh Mishra into illicit account ACC5118.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Dinesh Singh using mobile 9870900658 and co-accused Karan Chauhan at number 9946361787.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2958. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
