JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Ajay Tiwari, alias 'Pandit', who functioned as Syndicate Henchman.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-7314 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 6,680,000 were illegally transferred from victim Kunal Joshi (Account ACC9003) into illicit mule account ACC6024 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Hitec City Phase 2 and Hazratganj Market.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9814991479 held by Ajay Tiwari (alias Pandit) traversing cell towers in Hitec City Phase 2 and Hazratganj Market.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Ajay Tiwari (alias Pandit) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC6024 is confirmed.
