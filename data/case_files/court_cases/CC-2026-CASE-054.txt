IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-054
State Prosecution VERSUS Vijay Nair alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0054 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Vijay Nair, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Manoj Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Hitec City and New Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Deshmukh seized vehicle KA37MJ9955 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9844088261.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 900,000 transferred from account ACC5183 belonging to victim Ajay Gupta into illicit account ACC1153.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Vijay Nair using mobile 9844088261 and co-accused Manoj Khan at number 9971682508.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2210. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
