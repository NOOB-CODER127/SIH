IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-074
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Deshmukh alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0074 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Sunil Ahmed to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Hyderabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Joshi seized vehicle MH66CD6724 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9832566390.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,890,000 transferred from account ACC9363 belonging to victim Suresh Sharma into illicit account ACC2868.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Deshmukh using mobile 9832566390 and co-accused Sunil Ahmed at number 9920251466.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3419. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
