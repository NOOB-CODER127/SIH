IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-056
State Prosecution VERSUS Vijay Mehta alias Bunty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0056 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Vijay Mehta, known in criminal records as Bunty, co-operated with accomplice Dinesh Yadav to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle TS25JK5106 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9840885967.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,570,000 transferred from account ACC6772 belonging to victim Anil Patil into illicit account ACC4588.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Vijay Mehta using mobile 9840885967 and co-accused Dinesh Yadav at number 9991478884.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3240. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
