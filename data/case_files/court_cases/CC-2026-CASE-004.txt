IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-004
State Prosecution VERSUS Rahul Singh alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0004 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Rahul Singh, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Rohan Ahmed to execute unauthorized transactions in Charbagh Railway Station and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle TS89JK4598 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9831931511.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,600,000 transferred from account ACC7216 belonging to victim Prakash Mishra into illicit account ACC5422.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rahul Singh using mobile 9831931511 and co-accused Rohan Ahmed at number 9972043515.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6313. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
