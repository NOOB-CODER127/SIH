JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by ACP Crime Branch A.K. Varma centers on accused Manoj Gupta, alias 'Munna', who functioned as Financial Launderer.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-7538 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 2,910,000 were illegally transferred from victim Rajesh Malhotra (Account ACC9005) into illicit mule account ACC7753 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Vibhuti Khand and Charbagh Railway Station.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9825430990 held by Manoj Gupta (alias Munna) traversing cell towers in Vibhuti Khand and Charbagh Railway Station.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Manoj Gupta (alias Munna) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC7753 is confirmed.
