IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-016
State Prosecution VERSUS Amit Gupta alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0016 registered in connection with an investigation into phishing ring siphoning funds into mule accounts.
2. The primary suspect Amit Gupta, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Vijay Tiwari to execute unauthorized transactions in Bangalore and Mumbai.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Patil seized vehicle UP69CD9288 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9819736572.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,450,000 transferred from account ACC8508 belonging to victim Sanjay Sharma into illicit account ACC2638.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Amit Gupta using mobile 9819736572 and co-accused Vijay Tiwari at number 9911250299.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3170. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
