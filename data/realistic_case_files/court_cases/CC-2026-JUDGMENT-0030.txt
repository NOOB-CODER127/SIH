JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by ACP Crime Branch A.K. Varma centers on accused Vikram Shukla, alias 'Bunty', who functioned as Vehicle Syndicate Kingpin.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-2994 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 2,710,000 were illegally transferred from victim Priya Singh (Account ACC9002) into illicit mule account ACC2665 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Gomti Nagar Extension and Hitec City Phase 2.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9820448222 held by Vikram Shukla (alias Bunty) traversing cell towers in Gomti Nagar Extension and Hitec City Phase 2.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Vikram Shukla (alias Bunty) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC2665 is confirmed.
