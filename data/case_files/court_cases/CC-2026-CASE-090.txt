IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-090
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Ahmed alias Raja & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0090 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Ahmed, known in criminal records as Raja, co-operated with accomplice Arun Rao to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Hazratganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mishra seized vehicle KA78MJ7981 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9854012664.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 950,000 transferred from account ACC5843 belonging to victim Suresh Sharma into illicit account ACC6021.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Ahmed using mobile 9854012664 and co-accused Arun Rao at number 9998707076.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-8272. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
