IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-023
State Prosecution VERSUS Dinesh Sheikh alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0023 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Dinesh Sheikh, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Kumar to execute unauthorized transactions in New Delhi and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Shukla seized vehicle TS43JK6143 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9836445607.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,490,000 transferred from account ACC8066 belonging to victim Sunil Mishra into illicit account ACC2146.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Dinesh Sheikh using mobile 9836445607 and co-accused Ramesh Kumar at number 9958884978.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3041. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
