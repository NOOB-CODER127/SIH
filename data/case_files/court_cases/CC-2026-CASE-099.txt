IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-099
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Rao alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0099 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Rao, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Gomti Nagar.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Pandey seized vehicle DL98EF9437 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9851954112.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,970,000 transferred from account ACC6658 belonging to victim Imran Sharma into illicit account ACC7532.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Rao using mobile 9851954112 and co-accused Rajesh Khan at number 9952857920.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2739. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
