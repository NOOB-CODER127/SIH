IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-080
State Prosecution VERSUS Vikram Singh alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0080 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Vikram Singh, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Prakash Sheikh to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Gomti Nagar.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle MH99JK9626 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9830796389.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 290,000 transferred from account ACC5846 belonging to victim Anil Sheikh into illicit account ACC2657.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Vikram Singh using mobile 9830796389 and co-accused Prakash Sheikh at number 9920356922.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-6517. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
