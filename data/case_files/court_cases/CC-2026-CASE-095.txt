IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-095
State Prosecution VERSUS Karan Deshmukh alias Bhaiya & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0095 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Karan Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Bhaiya, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Hazratganj and Aminabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Tiwari seized vehicle KA74AB3053 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9863440127.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,720,000 transferred from account ACC9612 belonging to victim Arun Ahmed into illicit account ACC7730.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Karan Deshmukh using mobile 9863440127 and co-accused Sanjay Mishra at number 9974245343.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2215. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
