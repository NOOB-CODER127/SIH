IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-094
State Prosecution VERSUS Amit Singh alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0094 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Amit Singh, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Salim Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Pune and Gomti Nagar.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mishra seized vehicle DL16CD3275 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9895590648.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,310,000 transferred from account ACC6922 belonging to victim Suresh Patil into illicit account ACC9335.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Amit Singh using mobile 9895590648 and co-accused Salim Mishra at number 9933366099.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9094. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
