IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-033
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Deshmukh alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0033 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Deshmukh, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Sanjay Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Delhi and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle MH04EF7149 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9825372341.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,130,000 transferred from account ACC6096 belonging to victim Prakash Nair into illicit account ACC2771.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Deshmukh using mobile 9825372341 and co-accused Sanjay Mehta at number 9931980274.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-4249. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
