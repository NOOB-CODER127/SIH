IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-104
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Tiwari alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0104 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Tiwari, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Sunil Tiwari to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Banjara Hills.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sharma seized vehicle MH69GH5911 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9892745294.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,530,000 transferred from account ACC6511 belonging to victim Arun Sharma into illicit account ACC9311.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Tiwari using mobile 9892745294 and co-accused Sunil Tiwari at number 9950162553.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1272. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
