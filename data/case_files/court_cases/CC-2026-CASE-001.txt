IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-001
State Prosecution VERSUS Rohan Gupta alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0001 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Rohan Gupta, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Ajay Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and Lucknow.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Mishra seized vehicle MH78AB4257 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9813999315.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,760,000 transferred from account ACC4582 belonging to victim Salim Deshmukh into illicit account ACC4811.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rohan Gupta using mobile 9813999315 and co-accused Ajay Verma at number 9922575562.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9928. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
