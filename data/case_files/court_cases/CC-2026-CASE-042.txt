IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-042
State Prosecution VERSUS Arun Yadav alias Don & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0042 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Arun Yadav, known in criminal records as Don, co-operated with accomplice Deepak Sheikh to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sheikh seized vehicle UP53GH8699 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9833328859.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,590,000 transferred from account ACC2229 belonging to victim Rohan Ahmed into illicit account ACC3564.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Arun Yadav using mobile 9833328859 and co-accused Deepak Sheikh at number 9984087145.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1534. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
