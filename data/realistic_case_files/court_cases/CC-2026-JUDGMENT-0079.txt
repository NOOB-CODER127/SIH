JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Inspector Cyber Cell Priya Rao centers on accused Ajay Tiwari, alias 'Pandit', who functioned as Syndicate Henchman.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-9120 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 5,070,000 were illegally transferred from victim Sunil Agrawal (Account ACC9006) into illicit mule account ACC3518 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Banjara Hills Road No 12 and Connaught Place Inner Circle.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9855021254 held by Ajay Tiwari (alias Pandit) traversing cell towers in Banjara Hills Road No 12 and Connaught Place Inner Circle.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Ajay Tiwari (alias Pandit) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC3518 is confirmed.
