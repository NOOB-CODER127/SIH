JUDGMENT
1. This appeal arises out of FIR No. 2026-0041 registered at Police Station Hazratganj, Lucknow. 
The prosecution case is that the accused Vikram Shukla (P001), alias Bunty, along with Ramesh Yadav (P002), alias Chotu, 
operated an organized vehicle theft syndicate operating across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi.
2. The investigating officer Inspector S.K. Pandey recovered vehicle UP32CD7788 from the possession of Manoj Gupta (P020), alias Munna, at Aminabad market.
3. Financial analysis revealed illegal transactions amounting to Rs. 14,50,000 transferred from account ACC9001 of victim Rohan Mehta to mule account ACC3003 managed by Manoj Gupta.
4. Surveillance records show call detail record (CDR) interactions between mobile 9876543210 registered under Bunty and phone 9933557788 held by Manoj Gupta.
5. Co-accused Salim Ahmed (P003), alias Salim Bhai, was intercepted at Charbagh Railway Station with stolen vehicle UP32AB4455.
6. The suspicious financial transactions were flagged under indicator STR-2026-0033. Court finds substantial evidence to uphold conviction under Section 379/411 IPC.
