IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-014
State Prosecution VERSUS Anil Tiwari alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0014 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Anil Tiwari, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Suresh Sheikh to execute unauthorized transactions in Lucknow and Charbagh Railway Station.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Yadav seized vehicle MH68CD1931 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9852091325.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,700,000 transferred from account ACC1821 belonging to victim Dinesh Mishra into illicit account ACC8811.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Anil Tiwari using mobile 9852091325 and co-accused Suresh Sheikh at number 9917672593.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2312. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
