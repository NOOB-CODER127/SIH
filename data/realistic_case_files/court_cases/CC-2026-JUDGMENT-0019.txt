JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Ramesh Yadav, alias 'Chotu', who functioned as Lieutenant / Extortionist.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-6333 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 5,890,000 were illegally transferred from victim Rajesh Malhotra (Account ACC9005) into illicit mule account ACC1552 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Banjara Hills Road No 12 and Bandra Kurla Complex.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9894122067 held by Ramesh Yadav (alias Chotu) traversing cell towers in Banjara Hills Road No 12 and Bandra Kurla Complex.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Ramesh Yadav (alias Chotu) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC1552 is confirmed.
