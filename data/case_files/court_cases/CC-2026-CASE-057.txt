IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-057
State Prosecution VERSUS Ramesh Patil alias Doctor & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0057 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Ramesh Patil, known in criminal records as Doctor, co-operated with accomplice Ramesh Deshmukh to execute unauthorized transactions in Bangalore and Aminabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Singh seized vehicle DL70EF9697 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9853851498.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,660,000 transferred from account ACC3877 belonging to victim Ajay Ahmed into illicit account ACC4289.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ramesh Patil using mobile 9853851498 and co-accused Ramesh Deshmukh at number 9965767881.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5465. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
