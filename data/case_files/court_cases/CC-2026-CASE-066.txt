IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-066
State Prosecution VERSUS Deepak Verma alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0066 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Deepak Verma, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Karan Sheikh to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and Hyderabad.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Kumar seized vehicle HR55EF5176 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9870867592.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,010,000 transferred from account ACC7815 belonging to victim Sanjay Gupta into illicit account ACC2557.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Deepak Verma using mobile 9870867592 and co-accused Karan Sheikh at number 9963516659.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3500. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
