IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-072
State Prosecution VERSUS Ajay Mehta alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0072 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Ajay Mehta, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Amit Verma to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Gomti Nagar.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Shukla seized vehicle MH86EF2180 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9861030579.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,130,000 transferred from account ACC4770 belonging to victim Anil Kumar into illicit account ACC9196.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ajay Mehta using mobile 9861030579 and co-accused Amit Verma at number 9981481992.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1693. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
