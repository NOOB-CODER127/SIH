IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-063
State Prosecution VERSUS Rohan Tiwari alias Salim Bhai & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0063 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Rohan Tiwari, known in criminal records as Salim Bhai, co-operated with accomplice Karan Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Gomti Nagar and Lucknow.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Rao seized vehicle DL34AB3076 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9858082464.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,890,000 transferred from account ACC6304 belonging to victim Deepak Shukla into illicit account ACC1258.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rohan Tiwari using mobile 9858082464 and co-accused Karan Mehta at number 9941934639.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-7897. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
