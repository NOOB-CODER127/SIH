IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-040
State Prosecution VERSUS Karan Patil alias Shorty & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0040 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Karan Patil, known in criminal records as Shorty, co-operated with accomplice Ajay Khan to execute unauthorized transactions in Aminabad and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle UP33GH2869 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9874251933.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,300,000 transferred from account ACC6582 belonging to victim Vikram Deshmukh into illicit account ACC4020.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Karan Patil using mobile 9874251933 and co-accused Ajay Khan at number 9969118721.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-2070. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
