IN THE SESSIONS COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-093
State Prosecution VERSUS Prakash Khan alias Pandit & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0093 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Prakash Khan, known in criminal records as Pandit, co-operated with accomplice Vijay Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Charbagh Railway Station and Hitec City.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Verma seized vehicle DL16CD2213 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9838799154.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,410,000 transferred from account ACC5678 belonging to victim Rohan Mehta into illicit account ACC9047.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Prakash Khan using mobile 9838799154 and co-accused Vijay Mishra at number 9992124765.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-3828. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
