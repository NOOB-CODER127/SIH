IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-009
State Prosecution VERSUS Ajay Tiwari alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0009 registered in connection with an investigation into vehicle theft syndicate operating across state borders.
2. The primary suspect Ajay Tiwari, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Anil Mishra to execute unauthorized transactions in Pune and Delhi.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Rao seized vehicle TS40GH7118 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9866792612.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 2,340,000 transferred from account ACC9835 belonging to victim Rohan Chauhan into illicit account ACC4295.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Ajay Tiwari using mobile 9866792612 and co-accused Anil Mishra at number 9938427073.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9479. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
