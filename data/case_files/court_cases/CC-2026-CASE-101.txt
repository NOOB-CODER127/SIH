IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-101
State Prosecution VERSUS Sanjay Khan alias PK & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0101 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Sanjay Khan, known in criminal records as PK, co-operated with accomplice Anil Shukla to execute unauthorized transactions in Hitec City and Aliganj.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Ahmed seized vehicle UP37GH8710 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9838876195.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 1,560,000 transferred from account ACC6781 belonging to victim Vijay Chauhan into illicit account ACC1797.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Sanjay Khan using mobile 9838876195 and co-accused Anil Shukla at number 9988295121.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-9793. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
