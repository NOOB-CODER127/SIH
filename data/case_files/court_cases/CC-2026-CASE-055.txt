IN THE SPECIAL CYBER CRIME COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-055
State Prosecution VERSUS Dinesh Patil alias Captain & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0055 registered in connection with an investigation into extortion racket threatening local traders and businessmen.
2. The primary suspect Dinesh Patil, known in criminal records as Captain, co-operated with accomplice Rajesh Mehta to execute unauthorized transactions in Banjara Hills and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Pandey seized vehicle MH46JK5847 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9874855088.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 3,680,000 transferred from account ACC4886 belonging to victim Suresh Sheikh into illicit account ACC7248.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Dinesh Patil using mobile 9874855088 and co-accused Rajesh Mehta at number 9924314759.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-5837. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
