IN THE PMLA SPECIAL COURT, NEW DELHI
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-047
State Prosecution VERSUS Rajesh Yadav alias Munna & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0047 registered in connection with an investigation into inter-state illicit substance distribution network.
2. The primary suspect Rajesh Yadav, known in criminal records as Munna, co-operated with accomplice Sunil Singh to execute unauthorized transactions in Mumbai and Lucknow.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Nair seized vehicle HR57MJ8941 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9837321124.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 720,000 transferred from account ACC9998 belonging to victim Anil Pandey into illicit account ACC3146.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Rajesh Yadav using mobile 9837321124 and co-accused Sunil Singh at number 9945843681.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1472. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
