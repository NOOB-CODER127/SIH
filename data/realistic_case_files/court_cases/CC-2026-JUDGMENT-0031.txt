JUDGMENT & ORDER

1. This appeal is directed against the order of the Special Court in connection with organized crime syndicate operations across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi. The prosecution case presented by Sub-Inspector Vikram Singh centers on accused Deepak Verma, alias 'Doctor', who functioned as Logistics Driver.

2. FINANCIAL TRAIL & FORENSIC EVIDENCE:
The Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) generated Suspicious Transaction Report reference STR-2026-5550 regarding anomalous fund flows. Investigation established that extortion funds totaling Rs. 5,640,000 were illegally transferred from victim Ananya Sharma (Account ACC9004) into illicit mule account ACC9088 controlled by the accused syndicate operating out of Connaught Place Inner Circle and Banjara Hills Road No 12.

3. CALL DETAIL RECORD (CDR) & CELL TOWER EVIDENCE:
Technical analysis of Call Detail Records submitted by law enforcement proves continuous voice and data interactions on mobile number 9891339186 held by Deepak Verma (alias Doctor) traversing cell towers in Connaught Place Inner Circle and Banjara Hills Road No 12.

4. CONCLUSION & RULING:
Having examined the digital forensics, bank account ledgers, and witness testimonies, the Court finds overwhelming evidence establishing conspiracy under IPC Section 120-B and money laundering under PMLA Section 3/4. Bail application of accused Deepak Verma (alias Doctor) is hereby REJECTED. Direct freeze of account ACC9088 is confirmed.
