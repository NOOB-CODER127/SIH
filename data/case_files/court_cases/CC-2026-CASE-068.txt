IN THE SPECIAL NCB COURT
Case No. CC-2026-CASE-068
State Prosecution VERSUS Suresh Singh alias Chotu & Ors.

JUDGMENT
1. This proceeding arises out of FIR No. FIR-2026-0068 registered in connection with an investigation into hawala network laundering illegal proceeds via shell companies.
2. The primary suspect Suresh Singh, known in criminal records as Chotu, co-operated with accomplice Dinesh Chauhan to execute unauthorized transactions in Aliganj and Pune.
3. Investigating officer Inspector Sheikh seized vehicle HR74CD8030 and recovered mobile device associated with phone number 9824524825.
4. Financial analysis by law enforcement established illegal transactions totaling Rs. 4,420,000 transferred from account ACC7022 belonging to victim Ajay Khan into illicit account ACC2882.
5. Call detail records show 45 inter-state communications between primary suspect Suresh Singh using mobile 9824524825 and co-accused Dinesh Chauhan at number 9956991504.
6. The Financial Intelligence Unit issued compliance report under flag STR-2026-1430. The tribunal finds sufficient grounds under IPC Section 420/120B to charge the accused.
